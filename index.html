Important: Before You Sell

To complete any sale with Smithfield Coin Company, the following two payments are required from the seller, and they must be paid upfront:

1. Delivery Fee — Paid Upfront

The seller pays the delivery fee upfront so we can arrange the secure, insured transport of the coins to us. This fee is between R700 and R1,500, depending on the package.

2. Certificate Fee — R600 (Compulsory)

Every sale requires a compulsory authenticity certificate of R600. This certificate confirms the coin's value and protects both parties. No certificate, no sale.

Both payments must be settled before we can proceed with any transaction. We'll explain everything clearly on WhatsApp.
